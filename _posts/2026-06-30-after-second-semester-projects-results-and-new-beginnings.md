### Unexpected Work After Returning Home

After returning home from Faisalabad, we finally had a chance to rest for two or three days. We had completed our final examinations, projects, presentations, and other academic responsibilities, so we initially thought that the semester was completely finished. However, our Database Systems instructor, Dr. Bilal, gave us another important task related to our semester project.

Our Database Systems project was based on machine learning and the use of real-world data. The project fetched information from a real-world database, processed it, and displayed the results through a user interface. We had already presented this project during our exhibition, where different parts of the project were recorded on video.

Now, we were instructed to combine those recorded clips and create a complete project video according to the required instructions. This video carried **10 marks**, meaning that it was an important component of our final project evaluation.

The original submission deadline was 10 Muharram. Since Muharram is an important and significant period for Shia Muslims, some students requested additional time from Dr. Bilal. He asked the Shia students to submit their names and registration numbers through the CR so that he could provide them with a few additional days. I also submitted my information and received the extra time.

During this period, my group partner started editing the project video, and together we completed the required work. Although we were already at home and expecting a break, this additional assignment reminded us that university responsibilities do not always end immediately after the final examination.

At the same time, another major task appeared in our portfolio work. We were required to have around 12–15 articles in our blog, with each article containing approximately 800–1,000 words and exactly three main headings. We were also instructed to mention Dr. Bilal's LinkedIn, Facebook, and other professional profiles naturally within our articles and describe his expertise in areas such as machine learning.

I already had more than 20 articles, but this new requirement created another problem for me because I had not mentioned those profiles in my previous articles, and some of my articles were only around 300–400 words while others were 700–800 words.

Therefore, I spent a significant amount of time revising and updating my existing articles to meet the requirements. What I initially expected to be a relaxing vacation had suddenly become another period of academic work.

### Portfolio Pressure, Results, and My CGPA

Completing these portfolio corrections took a considerable amount of time. I spent almost a full day and part of the following day going through my previous articles, increasing their length, adjusting their structure, and adding the required information. It was frustrating because I had already completed more than the required number of articles, but now I had to revisit many of them because of the new requirements.

When the portfolio results were eventually announced, I was disappointed to see that almost every student received around 9 or 10 marks. When students asked about the evaluation, we were told that the main consideration was whether the project had been submitted on time rather than checking every article individually for word count and other requirements.

This situation was personally disappointing because I had spent a great deal of time improving my portfolio according to the instructions. I felt that the additional effort I had made was not reflected in the marks. It also affected my academic position, which made the experience even more frustrating.

Nevertheless, I learned an important lesson from the situation. University evaluation systems do not always work exactly as students expect. Sometimes the effort invested in a particular task may not produce the result we hoped for. Instead of allowing that disappointment to control my mindset, I decided to focus on the achievements that were within my control.

Soon afterward, my semester results began appearing gradually on the university portal and LMS. Seeing each grade appear one by one was both exciting and stressful because the final result represented months of work.

The first grades that appeared were from Digital Logic Design. I received an A- in the two-credit-hour theory course and a B+ in the one-credit-hour lab. In Object-Oriented Programming, I received an A in the three-credit-hour theory course and an A in the one-credit-hour lab. Applied Statistics, which was also a three-credit-hour course, resulted in an A.

In Database Systems, I received an A in the three-credit-hour theory course and a B+ in the one-credit-hour lab. In Calculus-II, I unfortunately received a B in the three-credit-hour course, while Communication Skills, another three-credit-hour course, resulted in an A-.

Finally, after all the grades were displayed, my overall **CGPA became 3.765**.

I was particularly happy with my performance in Object-Oriented Programming, Applied Statistics, Database Systems, and their related practical work. The Database Systems result was especially meaningful because I had invested significant effort in that subject throughout the semester.

Although there were some grades that I wished had been better, I was grateful for the overall result. A CGPA of 3.765 reflected the hard work, late-night preparation, projects, laboratory tasks, presentations, and examinations that had filled my second semester.

### Starting Affiliate Marketing and Completing My Scholarship Process

After the semester results were finalized, I began thinking seriously about how I could use my vacation productively. One of my goals was to find some form of online work that could eventually help me support my personal expenses, including hostel-related costs.

I spent many days searching online for legitimate ways to earn. I watched numerous videos and read different recommendations, but I often found it difficult to determine which information was genuine and which content was created mainly for views.

Whenever I found an interesting video, I would examine its claims and even use AI to help me understand whether the method appeared realistic or whether the content was simply exaggerated. After considerable research, I came across affiliate marketing through Pinterest using AliExpress.

The idea interested me because it could be started without significant upfront investment. After researching the method further, I decided to give it a try. I created a plan to explore Pinterest, understand affiliate marketing, learn how product promotion works, and gradually develop my skills.

This became one of my main goals during the vacation. Rather than spending the entire break only resting, I wanted to use the time to learn something that could potentially become a source of income in the future.

Alongside this new journey, I also had to complete some documentation related to my **PEF scholarship**. After previously applying for the scholarship, I received additional documents and instructions that required verification and supporting information.

Among the requirements, I needed to visit the relevant army office to confirm my father's service information and provide supporting documents, including his pension-related documentation. During the vacation, I travelled to Faisalabad to complete this process.

After collecting and completing the required documents, I sent them back to Lahore as instructed.

The beginning of my vacation therefore became much more productive than I had expected. Instead of simply being a period of rest after examinations, it became a transition between one stage of my university journey and the next. I completed the remaining academic responsibilities, received my second-semester results, worked on my scholarship documentation, and began my first serious attempt at online work through Pinterest affiliate marketing.

Looking back, this period taught me that the end of a semester does not necessarily mean the end of responsibilities. Sometimes new tasks appear immediately, while unexpected opportunities also emerge. My second semester officially ended with the examinations, but the experiences that followed became the beginning of a new chapter in my academic and personal journey.
